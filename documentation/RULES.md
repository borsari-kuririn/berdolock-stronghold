# Berdolock's Stronghold — Basic Rules

> Solo dungeon-crawl RPG set in the world of Atrúsia.
> Rules based on the first minimalist edition.

---

## 1. The Basics

You are an adventurer entering Berdolock's Stronghold in search of riches and glory.
The game is run by yourself, using dice and tables to generate the unknown.

**Required materials:**
- 2d6, 1d8, 1d12
- Paper and pencil
- This book

---

## 2. Character Creation

### Attributes

Roll **2d6** for each attribute and record the result:

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Strength (STR)** | Physical power and combat ability |
| **Agility (AGI)** | Speed, dodging, and reflexes |
| **Intelligence (INT)** | Knowledge, magic, and perception |
| **Endurance (END)** | Stamina and hit points |

### Hit Points (HP)

**Max HP = END × 2**

### Inventory

The character can carry up to **STR** items. Bulky items take up 2 slots.

---

## 3. Tests

When the outcome of an action is uncertain, roll **2d6** and compare to the relevant attribute:

| Result | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| ≤ attribute | **Success** |
| > attribute | **Failure** |

**Difficulty:**
- **Easy:** roll with advantage (roll 3d6, discard the highest)
- **Normal:** roll 2d6
- **Hard:** roll with disadvantage (roll 3d6, discard the lowest)

---

## 4. Exploration

The stronghold is divided into **rooms**. Upon entering each new room, roll on the **Encounter Table (1d8)**:

| 1d8 | Event |
|-----|-------|
| 1–3 | Empty room |
| 4–5 | Trap (AGI test to avoid 1d6 damage) |
| 6–7 | Monster (see monster table) |
| 8 | Treasure |

After every **4 rooms explored**, advance one **Danger** level (max 3).
Each Danger level increases enemy damage dice by one step: d4 → d6 → d8 → d10.

---

## 5. Combat

Combat happens in **turns**. Who acts first is determined by an **AGI** test.

### Player Turn
1. Declare your action (attack, defend, flee, use item)
2. Attack: roll 2d6 vs. STR. Success → roll damage (**1d6 + STR/4**)

### Monster Turn
- The monster attacks automatically; roll the monster's damage die and subtract from your HP.

### Fleeing
- Test **AGI**. Success: you escape. Failure: the monster attacks before you can run.

### Death
- At **0 HP**, the character dies. Create a new one to continue the quest.

## 7. Treasures (Basic Table, 1d6)

| 1d6 | Treasure |
|-----|----------|
| 1–2 | Coins (1d6 × 10 gold) |
| 3 | Healing potion (restores 1d6 HP) |
| 4 | Magic weapon (+1 to damage) |
| 5 | Light armor (reduces incoming damage by 1) |
| 6 | Berdolock's Artifact (special effect, to be defined) |

---

## 8. Progression

After defeating **3 monsters**, the character gains +1 to an attribute of their choice (max 12).

---

## 9. Objective

Reach **Berdolock's Throne Room** (room 20) and defeat the lord of the stronghold.

**Berdolock:**
- HP: 30
- Damage: 1d12
- Special ability: Once per combat, ignores one incoming damage roll.
